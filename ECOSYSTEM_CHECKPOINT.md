This is a **Personal AI Memory Engine** built over months. It consists of:
- **Backend API** (FastAPI) for querying and serving memory.
- **Agents & Brain** for AI reasoning, classification, and semantic search.
- **Knowledge Graphs & SQLite DBs** storing tens of thousands of data points.
- **Portfolio & Frontend** components for public-facing interaction.
